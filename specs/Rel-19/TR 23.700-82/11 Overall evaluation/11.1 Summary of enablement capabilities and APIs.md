# 11.1 Summary of enablement capabilities and APIs

Various solutions proposed in the present document aim 1) defining new analytics to be supported by ADAES or introducing or 2) introducing a new SEAL service (AIMLE, ML Repository) to support AI/ML services for vertical use cases. This clause provides a summary of the capabilities as well as the corresponding potential enablement APIs.
