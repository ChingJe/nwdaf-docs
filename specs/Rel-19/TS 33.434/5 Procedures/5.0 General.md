# 5.0 General

The security procedures in this clause also applies to SEALDD as specified in TS 23.433 \[30\]. In the SEALDD scenario, the SEAL client, SEAL server and SEAL service are replaced by the SEALDD client, SEALDD server and SEALDD service, respectively.
