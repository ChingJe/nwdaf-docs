# 4 Architectural Assumptions and Requirements

The present study will not consider service-based interfaces with RAN and with UE.

The architecture for the present study shall comply with the existing NWDAF framework as specified in TS 23.288 \[5\], and 5GS framework as specified in TS 23.501 \[2\], TS 23.502 \[3\] and TS 23.503 \[4\].

The architecture for the present study shall comply with the existing Location Service Architecture as specified in TS 23.273 \[7\].

NOTE 1: The study will consider the related work done by SA WG5, CT WG4 and reuse it when possible.

NOTE 2: Security aspects are to be addressed by SA WG3.

Regarding AI/ML cross-domain coordination aspects, work will be based on the possible requirements defined by RAN WGs considering the conclusions in TR 38.843 \[6\].

NOTE 3: UE data collection, model delivery and transfer to the UE and model identification/management are not within the scope of the study.
