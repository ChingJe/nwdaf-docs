# 6.23.3 Impacts on services, entities and interfaces

**AF:**

\- Support for new Naf service operations for training.

**NWDAF with MTLF:**

\- Updates Nnwdaf_MLModelTraining service to e.g. provide intermediate results.

\- Updates Nnwdaf_MLModelTraining to include a new service operation to notify the participants in the VFL training that a trained ML Model is available.

\- New Nnwdaf_MLModelInference service to e.g. provide intermediate inference results.

**NEF:**

\- Support for new Nnef service operations for training.

Editor's note: Whether additional impacted NFs or NF services is FFS.
