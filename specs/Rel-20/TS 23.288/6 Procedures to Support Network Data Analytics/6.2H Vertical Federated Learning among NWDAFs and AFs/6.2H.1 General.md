# 6.2H.1 General

This clause specifies procedures for Vertical Federated learning where AFs and NWDAF can can either act as VFL servers or VFL clients. Procedures for registration and discovery, for VFL training preparation, for VFL training, and for VFL inference are covered.

Both the VFL server and VFL client store the VFL model after finishing the VFL training process and use the same VFL local model to perform the VFL inference later based on the VFL correlation ID. The differences between the VFL training and inference are that for the inference there is no check of the labels, nor any server intermediate model training information are sent and as such only client intermediate results are sent to the server.
