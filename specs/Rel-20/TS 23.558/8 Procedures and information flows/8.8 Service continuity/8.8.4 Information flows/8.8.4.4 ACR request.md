# 8.8.4.4 ACR request

Table 8.8.4.4-1 describes information elements for the ACR request sent either from the EEC to the S-EES or T-EES, or by the S-EAS to the S-EES. In the EAS bundle case, the ACR request is sent by the main S-EES to its associated S-EES(s).

<p>NOTE 9: This IE may be included when the ACR is decided and executed for service continuity planning for a predicted/expected UE location.</p>
<p>NOTE 10: The IE shall be present when the action is ACR modification to identify the ACR to be modified.</p>
<p>NOTE 11: The IE is present when the EEC informs S-EES(s) with applicable bundled T-EAS(s) information for the direct bundle EAS case or when the main S-EES informs its associated S-EES(s) with applicable bundle T-EAS(s) information for the direct bundle EAS case.</p>
