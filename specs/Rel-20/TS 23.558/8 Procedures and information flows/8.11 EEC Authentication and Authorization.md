# 8.11 EEC Authentication/Authorization


## 8.11.1 General

The architecture for enabling edge applications supports EEC authentication/authorization.

After the successful EEC authentication/authorization, the EEC acquires a valid security credential for EEC related procedures including service provisioning procedure, EEC registration procedure, EAS discovery procedure and ACR procedure. Detailed procedures are specified in 3GPP TS 33.558 \[23\].
