# 4 General


## 4.1 Overview

Artificial intelligence machine learning enablement (AIMLE) SEAL services enable the AIMLE server to communicate with the AIMLE client over the AI/ML-UU reference points. The detailed specification of AIMLE is provided in 3GPP TS 23.482 \[4\].

The present document specifies the APIs in detail, needed to support the AIMLE services offered by the AIMLE client and the AIMLE services offered by the AIMLE server.
