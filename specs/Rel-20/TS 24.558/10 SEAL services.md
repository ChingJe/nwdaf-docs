# 10 SEAL services

The EEL can utilize following SEAL services to support EEL services:

a\) notification management as specified in 3GPP TS 24.542 \[10\] to receive notifications; and the EEC shall:

1\) initiate the notification channel creation procedures on SNM-C as specified in clause 6.2.2.1 in 3GPP TS 24.542 \[10\] to obtain the Notification Target Address (e.g. URL) and use it in the subscribe methods shared to EES and ECS;

2\) handle the notification messages received from SNM-C as specified in clause 6.2.3 in 3GPP TS 24.542 \[10\]; and

3\) initiate the notification channel deletion procedures on SNM-C as specified in clause 6.2.4.1 in 3GPP TS 24.542 \[10\].
