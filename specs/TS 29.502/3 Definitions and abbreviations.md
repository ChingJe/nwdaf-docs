# 3 Definitions and abbreviations

## 3.1 Definitions

For the purposes of the present document, the terms and definitions given in 3GPP TR 21.905 \[1\] and the following apply. A term defined in the present document takes precedence over the definition of the same term, if any, in 3GPP TR 21.905 \[1\].

**Alternative S-NSSAI:** Indicating a compatible S-NSSAI for an S-NSSAI in the Allowed NSSAI that the AMF uses to replace an S-NSSAI when the S-NSSAI is not available or congested, as specified in 3GPP TS 23.501 \[2\].
