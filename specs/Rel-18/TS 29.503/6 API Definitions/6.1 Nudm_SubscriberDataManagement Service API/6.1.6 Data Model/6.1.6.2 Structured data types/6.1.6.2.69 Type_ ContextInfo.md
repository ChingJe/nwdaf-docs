# 6.1.6.2.69 Type: ContextInfo

Table 6.1.6.2.69-1: Definition of type ContextInfo

| Attribute name | Data type     | P   | Cardinality | Description                                                                                                                                   |
|----------------|---------------|-----|-------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| origHeaders    | array(string) | O   | 1..N        | Headers received by the UDM from NFs consuming Nudm services. The encoding of the header shall comply with clause 6.3 of IETF RFC 9110 \[50\] |
| requestHeaders | array(string) | O   | 1..N        | Headers sent by the UDM to NFs (e.g. AMF). The encoding of the header shall comply with clause 6.3 of IETF RFC 9110 \[50\]                    |
