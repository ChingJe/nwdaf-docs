# 6.1.6.3.11 Enumeration: EpsInterworkingIndication

The enumeration EpsInterworkingIndication indicates whether and how the PDU session will possibly be moved to EPS or EPC/ePDG.

Table 6.1.6.3.11-1: Enumeration EpsInterworkingIndication

<table>
<colgroup>
<col style="width: 38%" />
<col style="width: 61%" />
</colgroup>
<thead>
<tr class="header">
<th>Enumeration value</th>
<th>Description</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr class="odd">
<td>"NONE"</td>
<td><p>The PDU session cannot be moved to EPS and EPC/ePDG.</p>
<p>(NOTE)</p></td>
</tr>
<tr class="even">
<td>"WITH_N26"</td>
<td><p>The PDU session may possibly be moved to EPS or EPC/ePDG, with N26 interface supported during EPS interworking procedures.</p>
<p>This may correspond to:<br />
- a PDU session or an MA-PDU session with a 3GPP access; or<br />
- a MA PDU Session with a non-3GPP access for a UE registered to the same PLMN over both 3GPP and non-3GPP accesses, i.e. served by the same AMF for both accesses.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr class="odd">
<td>"WITHOUT_N26"</td>
<td><p>The PDU session may possibly be moved to EPS or EPC/ePDG, without N26 interface supported during EPS interworking procedures.</p>
<p>This may correspond to:<br />
- a PDU session or an MA-PDU session with a 3GPP access; or<br />
- a MA PDU Session with a non-3GPP access for a UE registered to the same PLMN over both 3GPP and non-3GPP accesses, i.e. served by the same AMF for both accesses.</p></td>
</tr>
<tr class="even">
<td>"IWK_NON_3GPP"</td>
<td>The PDU session via non-3GPP access may possibly be moved to EPS.</td>
</tr>
<tr class="odd">
<td colspan="2">NOTE: See clause 4.11.5 of 3GPP TS 23.502 [3] for the setting of the EpsInterworkingIndication by the AMF and its handling by the SMF.</td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>
