# 5.6 Feature negotiation

The optional features in table 6.1.8-1 of 3GPP TS 29.504 \[2\] are defined for the Nudr_DataRepository API. They shall be negotiated using the extensibility mechanism defined in clause 6.6 of 3GPP TS 29.500 \[8\].
