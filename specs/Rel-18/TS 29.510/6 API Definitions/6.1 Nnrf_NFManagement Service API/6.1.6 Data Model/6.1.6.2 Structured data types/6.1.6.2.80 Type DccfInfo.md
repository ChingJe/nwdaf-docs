<td>servingNfTypeList</td>
<td>array(NFType)</td>
<td>O</td>
<td>1..N</td>

<tr class="even">
<td>servingNfSetIdList</td>
<td>array(NfSetId)</td>
<td>O</td>
<td>1..N</td>

</tr>
<tr class="odd">
<td>taiList</td>
<td>array(Tai)</td>
<td>O</td>
<td>1..N</td>
<td>The list of TAIs the DCCF can serve. It may contain one or more non-3GPP access TAIs. The absence of both this attribute and the taiRangeList attribute indicates that the DCCF can be selected for any TAI in the serving network.</td>
</tr>
<tr class="even">
<td>taiRangeList</td>
<td>array(TaiRange)</td>
<td>O</td>
<td>1..N</td>
<td>The range of TAIs the DCCF can serve. It may contain one or more non-3GPP access TAI ranges. The absence of both this attribute and the taiList attribute indicates that the DCCF can be selected for any TAI in the serving network.</td>
</tr>
<tr class="odd">
<td>dataSubsRelocInd</td>
<td>boolean</td>
<td>O</td>
<td>0..1</td>
<td><p>When present, this IE shall indicate whether the DCCF supports relocation of data subscription:</p>
<p>- true: relocation of data subscription is supported by the DCCF</p>
<p>- false (default): relocation of data subscription is not supported by the DCCF.</p></td>
