# 19.4.2.12 UCMF

The UCMF FQDN shall be constructed as:

ucmf.epc.mnc\<MNC\>.mcc\<MCC\>.3gppnetwork.org

Where \<mcc\> and \<mnc\> are taken from the serving network identity.
